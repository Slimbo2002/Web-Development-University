The Environment

Our planet is amazing, let's keep it that way!

Why do we need to protect the environment?

The environment is our home! The air we breathe, the water we drink, and the food we eat all depend on it being healthy. When we don't look after it, things like climate change happen. That means hotter summers, wilder storms, and animals losing their homes. The good news? Small choices every day can make a huge difference!

But what can I do?

We can recycle!

Recycling is one of the easiest superpowers you have! When we recycle, we turn old stuff into new stuff without using as much energy. Did you know recycling just one glass bottle saves enough energy to power a lightbulb for 4 whole hours?

Remember the Three R's:
Reduce - Use less stuff in the first place.

Reuse - Use things again before throwing them away.

Recycle - If you can't reuse it, recycle it so it can become something brand new!

Recycling paper uses 40% less energy than making it from scratch. That means more trees stay standing in our forests!

We can protect natural resources!

Natural resources like water, wood, and sand don't last forever, so we need to be careful how we use them. Farming uses 70% of all the world's water, which means when we waste food, we're also wasting water that people in other countries desperately need.

Here's how you can help protect our resources:

Don't waste food - Only take what you'll actually eat! Food rotting in landfill releases a gas called methane, which is 25 times worse for the planet than car fumes.

Save water - Turn off the tap when brushing your teeth.

Avoid fast fashion - Clothes made cheaply and thrown away quickly use up huge amounts of resources. Buying second hand or choosing quality items that last longer is much better for the planet!
