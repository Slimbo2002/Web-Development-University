Your City

Every city makes choices, what would yours look like?

How does your city affect the environment?

Every city in the world has to make tough choices. Do you build a cheap coal power station that pollutes the air, or an expensive solar farm that's cleaner? Do you fill empty land with housing, or keep it as a park? These are called trade-offs

For example, electric cars are much greener to drive than petrol cars, but the batteries inside them are really bad to make! And wind farms produce clean energy, but they take up land that animals used to live on. There's rarely a perfect answer, which is what makes city planning so tricky.
